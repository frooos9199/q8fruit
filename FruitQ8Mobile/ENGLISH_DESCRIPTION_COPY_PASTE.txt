Welcome to FruitQ8 — a mobile app in Kuwait for ordering fresh fruits and vegetables from Fardha Market to your doorstep.

🍎 ABOUT FRUITQ8

FruitQ8 brings the Fardha Market experience to your smartphone. We connect you with seasonal produce selected from trusted vendors at Kuwait’s wholesale fruit and vegetable market. Now you can enjoy market-quality freshness without leaving your home.

🛒 KEY FEATURES

• EXTENSIVE PRODUCT CATALOG
Browse through hundreds of fresh fruits, vegetables, and produce items organized by category. Each product includes detailed descriptions, pricing, and high-quality images to help you make the perfect selection.

• BILINGUAL SUPPORT (ARABIC & ENGLISH)
Fully functional in both Arabic and English languages. Switch between languages seamlessly to shop in your preferred language.

• REAL-TIME INVENTORY
See what's available right now. Our inventory updates in real-time so you always know what's in stock at the market.

• FLEXIBLE ORDERING
Add items to your cart, adjust quantities, and review your order before checkout. Save favorite items for quick reordering.

• MULTIPLE PAYMENT OPTIONS
Pay securely using various payment methods including cash on delivery, credit/debit cards, and digital payment solutions.

• DELIVERY SCHEDULING
Choose your preferred delivery time slot. We offer flexible delivery windows to fit your schedule.

• ORDER TRACKING
Track your order status from confirmation to delivery. Receive notifications at every step of the process.

• ACCOUNT MANAGEMENT
Create your personal account to save delivery addresses, view order history, track active orders, and manage your profile information.

• PRODUCT DETAILS
View comprehensive product information including origin, quality grade, weight options, and storage recommendations.

1. Download the FruitQ8 app
2. Create your account

5. Select delivery address and time slot
6. Choose your payment method
7. Confirm order and track delivery
